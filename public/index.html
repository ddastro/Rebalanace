Project Overview

End-to-end demand forecasting for bike-share rebalancing · CityCycle Hackathon · Regression track

The problem

A bike-share operator arrives at 8 AM to find empty docks — and at 6 PM, full ones. Both are rebalancing failures. Dispatching trucks reactively costs time and money. Forecasting demand one hour ahead lets ops teams pre-position bikes before the imbalance hits, not after.

What we built
  • →
    UCI Bike Sharing Dataset · 17,379 hourly records from Washington D.C., January 2011 – December 2012.
  • →
    Chronological train/test split · First 18 months for training, last 6 months held out. No random shuffle.
  • →
    Three models compared · Same-hour-last-week naive baseline, Ridge regression, HistGradientBoosting with cyclical sin/cos encoding.
  • →
    Permutation-based explainability · Top demand drivers with plain-language operational guidance.
